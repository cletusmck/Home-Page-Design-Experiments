Bridge helps finance the milestones that move hotels and consumer brands forward.

A specialist business finance company

Every consequential business has a moment when the plan is ready and the capital is not. The hotel is designed. The order is signed. The improvement plan is due. What remains is the financing.

Bridge exists for that moment. We are the direct lender to consumer brands, funding the production, inventory, and fulfillment costs that arrive before retailer payment does.1 We are the direct lender for hotel new construction and property improvement plans.3 And for other hotel transactions, we evaluate, structure, and secure the appropriate capital, then manage the process through closing.4

We believe proof matters more than promises. So we will simply show you what we have financed, and let you judge the resemblance to your own situation.

For consumer brands
Bridge is the direct lender. We fund approved production, inventory, vendor, and fulfillment costs tied to upcoming retail orders, so your cash and equity stay free for growth. Subject to underwriting.
For hotel construction and PIPs
Bridge is the direct lender. We make the lending decision and provide the capital for new construction and brand-mandated property improvement plans, subject to underwriting and documentation.
For other hotel financing
Bridge evaluates the transaction, structures it to hold through diligence, identifies the appropriate capital, and stays accountable until documents are signed and funds are wired.

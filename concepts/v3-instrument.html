The process

Five steps. One accountable team.

01

Understand

We learn the business objective, the capital need, the timing, and the context that matters.

02

Evaluate fit

We assess the request against program and market realities, and the likely path becomes explicit: direct lending or financing secured by Bridge.

03

Structure

We develop the financing approach so expectations survive diligence, not just the first conversation.

04

Provide or secure capital

Bridge underwrites its direct programs, or engages the appropriate capital for other hotel transactions.

05

Close

We manage diligence, documentation, and communication until funds are wired. Closing remains subject to underwriting, approvals, and definitive documentation.
